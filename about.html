About Hush

Hush is a school prototype of a privacy-focused social network. The idea: people should be able to share and express themselves without being profiled by AI systems.

How it protects you

There is no server or database. Everything is saved in your own browser (localStorage). Passwords are hashed, no cookies or trackers are used, and you can delete everything on the Privacy page.

Limits

Because it is frontend only, accounts and posts exist only on one device, so users cannot see each other's posts across computers. A real version would need a secure backend.
